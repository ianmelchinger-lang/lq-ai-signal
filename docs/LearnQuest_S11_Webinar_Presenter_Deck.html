The four-part model

Four boxes. You'll fill them in for a real deal in twenty minutes.

AIWE-S-11W  ·  10 / 41
2

1 · The leak

What does the problem cost per period, today?
Volume × effort × loaded cost.
Keep the period small. Weekly sources easily.
AIWE-S-11W  ·  11 / 41
2

2 · The recoverable share

How much of the leak actually goes away?
Never all of it.
Almost always an assumption. Agree it out loud.
AIWE-S-11W  ·  12 / 41
2

3 · The delay

How long until a decision realistically lands?
Ask them. Do not guess.
Longer than your champion first says.

4 · The dated event

Is there a date inside that window?
Renewal, escalator, audit, a migration window closing.
A number argues. A date decides.

Build your figure

  1. Two minutes: pick one live deal that has gone quiet. Paste your notes raw.
  2. One minute: run the prompt. Do not edit the notes first.
  3. Seven minutes: source every input it filled. Check each quoted sentence.
  4. Two minutes: any quote that turns out to be you talking is Assumed, not Stated.
  5. Two minutes: say the plausibility fraction out loud. Do you flinch?
Write down every slot it left blank. That list is your next call, and it is the second deliverable of this hour.

Stuck?

  1. Model looks complete? Read the flags before the boxes.
  2. Quote turns out to be you talking? Assumed, not Stated.
  3. No hourly rate in your notes? It guessed one. Go and get the real one.
  4. No dated event? Check the contract they already sent you.
Two-minute warning at minute ten.
